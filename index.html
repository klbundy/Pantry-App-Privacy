Privacy Policy for RIQUO

Last updated: 10-03-2026

RIQUO (formerly Pantry AI) is a pantry, recipe, and shopping list app. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us). If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name (the first time you sign in, used as your display name) and an email address, which may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email". We never receive your Apple ID password.
  • Profile information (optional): a display name and profile photo, if you choose to add them.
  • Pantry and household data: pantry items you add manually, by voice, or by photo/receipt scanning, including quantities, prices, and expiration dates; shopping list items; and the shared pantries you create or join.
  • Recipes and cooking history: recipes you generate, import, or save, and meals you log.
  • Food preferences: dietary preferences, restrictions, and allergies you choose to provide.
  • AI usage counts: how many times each week you use each AI feature, used to apply weekly limits. We store the counts, not the content of your requests.
  • Subscription status: if you subscribe to RIQUO Plus, whether your subscription is active and when it renews or expires. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and app performance data, used to find and fix bugs.

Camera, Photos & Microphone

With your permission, the app uses your camera and photo library so you can scan receipts, pantry items, and recipe photos, and so you can choose a profile photo. With your permission, the app uses your microphone only while you hold the voice button to add pantry items by speaking them. The app never accesses your camera, photos, or microphone in the background.

AI Features and OpenAI

RIQUO's AI features are powered by OpenAI. Before any of your data is sent to OpenAI, the app asks for your permission. If you allow it, then when you use an AI feature the following is sent to OpenAI's API, through our server, to process that request:

  • Receipt, pantry, and recipe photos you scan or import (to identify items or extract recipe details)
  • Voice recordings you make with the voice button (to transcribe them into pantry items)
  • Your pantry item names, food preferences, and allergies (to generate or modify recipes that fit them)
  • Recipe names or instructions you type (for recipe search and "Ask AI to modify")

Nothing is sent to OpenAI unless you use an AI feature. Our server passes these requests to OpenAI and returns the result; it does not keep your photos or voice recordings. OpenAI processes this data under its API data usage policies, which do not use API data to train its models by default and may retain it for a limited time (up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring. If you choose not to allow AI processing, you can still add and manage items manually. You can turn AI processing off at any time in Settings → AI Processing; the app will ask again before sending anything.

How We Use Your Information

Only to provide the app's features: tracking your pantry, generating recipe suggestions, managing your shopping list and meal history, syncing your data across your devices and with members of your shared pantry, and fixing crashes and bugs. Allergies and dietary restrictions are used only to tailor recipe suggestions.

Data Sharing

If you join a shared pantry, other members of that pantry can see its pantry items and shopping list. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising or tracking.

Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers, which process data only to run the app:

  • Supabase for account sign-in, data storage, profile photo storage, and hosting the server that sends AI requests to OpenAI
  • Apple for Sign in with Apple, if you choose to use it
  • OpenAI for image recognition, voice transcription, and recipe generation (only after you allow it)
  • RevenueCat to manage RIQUO Plus subscriptions; it receives an anonymous account identifier and your purchase and subscription status from Apple or Google, not your name, email, or payment details
  • Render to host the server used by older versions of the app
  • Sentry for crash reporting and performance monitoring

Account & Data Deletion

To delete your RIQUO account and associated personal data:

  1. Open the RIQUO app and sign in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

If you no longer have the app installed, email keithbundy11@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted: your account credentials, profile (display name and photo), personal preferences, saved recipes, and meal tracking history, AI usage counts, and subscription status are permanently deleted immediately. If you signed in with Apple, RIQUO's access to your Apple ID is also revoked.

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a RIQUO Plus subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

What may be retained: if you were a member of a shared household pantry, pantry items you added remain visible to other members of that pantry (ownership is removed, but shared inventory data is not deleted, since it belongs to the household, not just you).

Children

RIQUO is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

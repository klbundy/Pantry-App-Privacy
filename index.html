Privacy Policy for Pantry AI

Last updated: 08-17-2026

Information We Collect

Account information (email address); pantry inventory data you add manually or via photo/receipt scanning; recipes you generate, import, or save; cooking history you log; shopping list items; and dietary preferences, restrictions, and allergies you provide.

Camera & Photo Access

With your permission, the app accesses your camera and photo library to let you scan receipts, pantry items, and recipe photos. These images are sent to OpenAI's API for processing (identifying items or extracting recipe details) and are not stored by OpenAI beyond what's necessary to generate a response.

How We Use Your Information

To provide core app functionality — tracking your pantry, generating personalized recipe suggestions, and managing your shopping list. Dietary restrictions and allergies you provide are used solely to filter AI-generated recipe suggestions.

Data Sharing

If you join a shared pantry, other members of that pantry can see the pantry's items, recipes, and shopping list. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for account authentication and data storage, and OpenAI's API for image recognition and recipe generation. Both process data solely to provide app functionality.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and associated personal data at any time from the app's Settings screen.
